# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue **20 days of paid vacation per year**, accruing at
1.67 days per month. Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in
advance and approved by your direct manager. Up to 5 unused vacation days may be
carried over into the following calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited.

In addition to vacation, employees receive **10 paid sick days** per year. Sick
days do not roll over. A doctor's note is required for any sick leave longer
than three consecutive days.

## Remote Work Policy

Acme Corp operates on a hybrid model. Employees are expected to be in the office
**at least two days per week**, typically Tuesday and Thursday. Fully remote
arrangements require VP approval and are reviewed annually. Home-office
equipment stipends of up to $500 are available once per two-year period.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within **30 days**
of being incurred. Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Meals during
business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Airfare should be booked in
economy class unless the flight exceeds six hours, in which case premium economy
is permitted.

## Code of Conduct

All employees must complete annual security and anti-harassment training.
Failure to complete required training within 30 days of assignment may result in
suspension of system access. Confidential company information must never be
shared outside the organization without written authorization.

## Parental Leave

Acme Corp provides **16 weeks of paid parental leave** for the primary caregiver
and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Leave must be taken within 12 months of
the birth or adoption of a child. Employees are eligible after 6 months of
continuous employment.
