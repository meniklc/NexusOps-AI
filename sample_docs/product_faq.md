# Acme Product FAQ

## Widget Pro

The **Widget Pro** is our flagship hardware unit, priced at $19.99 per unit. It
ships with a **12-month limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects. The
Widget Pro supports firmware updates over USB-C and is compatible with the Acme
Analytics platform.

### Return Policy

Hardware products, including the Widget Pro and Widget Mini, may be returned
within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund, provided they are in original
condition. Opened software licenses (such as Analytics Seats) are
**non-refundable** except where required by law. Return shipping is free for
defective items; the customer pays return shipping otherwise.

## Analytics Seat

An **Analytics Seat** is a per-user annual software license for the Acme
Analytics platform, priced at $149.00 per seat per year. Seats are billed
annually and auto-renew unless cancelled 30 days before the renewal date.
Volume discounts of 10% apply to orders of 50 seats or more.

## Support Gold

**Support Gold** is our premium support tier at $999.00 per year. It includes
**24/7 phone and email support**, a **4-hour response SLA** for critical issues,
and a dedicated technical account manager. Standard support (included free with
all products) offers business-hours email support with a 2-business-day SLA.

## Shipping

Orders in the NA and EMEA regions ship within 2 business days. APAC and LATAM
orders ship within 5 business days. Expedited shipping is available at checkout
for an additional fee. Bulk orders over 300 units may require additional lead
time.

## Data & Privacy

Customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Acme retains order data for
7 years for compliance purposes. Customers may request deletion of their
personal data by contacting privacy@acme.example, subject to legal retention
requirements.
